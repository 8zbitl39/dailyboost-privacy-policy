Last updated: August 9, 2026

DailyBoost respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the DailyBoost mobile application.

DailyBoost does not require you to create an account to use its core features.

If you enable notifications, DailyBoost may send reminders about your tasks and habits. You can disable notifications at any time through your device settings.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. We do not intentionally share your personal information with third parties unless required to provide a feature of the application or comply with applicable law.

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the application. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

5. Children's Privacy

DailyBoost is not specifically directed toward children under the applicable minimum age for providing consent to data processing in their country.
